## House style (match Posts #1 and #2)

Both existing blog heroes are **photorealistic environmental photographs** of
Singapore creative/marketing work scenes — bright natural window light, warm
neutral tones (white, timber, soft grey), real people at work, Singapore context
visible through windows (greenery, shophouse frames, muted skyline). Brand cyan
`#02BAF9` appears only as small incidental accents, never as a wash or effect.
There is **no illustration, no 3D, no UI graphics, no skyline-plus-icons
composite** — earlier drafts of this brief were wrong on that point.

## Template facts (so the composition works)

- The article page renders the title **above** the image in a centred text
  block — text is **not** overlaid on the image, so a title safe-area is a
  nice-to-have, not a hard requirement.
- The `/blog` listing crops this image to a wide **21:9** band (horizontal
  centre of the frame). Keep the key subjects within the central horizontal
  third so the crop never cuts heads or hands.
- Shown at up to ~1200px wide; must read at a glance as a small card too.

## 1. Generation Prompt (photorealistic)

```
Photorealistic editorial photograph, landscape 16:9, natural available light,
35mm lens look, shallow depth of field. For a Singapore marketing agency's blog.

SCENE: a small in-house marketing team — two or three people, business-casual,
plausibly Singaporean / Southeast Asian, late 20s to 40s — seated together at a
light timber meeting table in a bright, contemporary Singapore office, mid-
conversation as they review social media campaign performance and weigh an
agency's strategy. One person points at an open laptop; another holds a printed
one-page report or a notebook and pen; a tablet, a coffee cup and a couple of
printouts sit on the table. The body language reads as evaluating, comparing and
deciding — not casually scrolling a phone.

LIGHT & SETTING: warm daylight from a large window on the left; soft natural
shadows. Background softly out of focus: glass partition, a few real plants, a
muted view of Singapore greenery or low skyline. Calm, uncluttered upper-left
and centre-left area.

COLOUR: predominantly neutral — white walls, warm wood, soft grey, denim. Brand
cyan (#02BAF9) only as small incidental accents (a laptop lid, a chair detail, a
notebook cover, a lanyard). Cyan must never dominate or look like a filter.

COMPOSITION: subjects grouped toward the right / lower-right; keep faces and
hands within the central horizontal band of the frame. Leave the upper-left and
centre-left visually quiet (wall, soft light, out-of-focus window).

REALISM: authentic skin texture and expressions, real fabric wrinkles, minor
believable desk clutter, true-to-life colour, subtle film grain. Candid, not
posed.

DO NOT INCLUDE: any text, letters or numbers; readable or lit screen content
(keep screens dim, angled away or out of focus); logos, watermarks or brand
names; glowing holograms, floating icons, HUD or dashboard graphics; 3D render
or illustration look; plastic AI faces, exaggerated smiles, symmetrical stock-
photo posing; neon or futuristic styling; handshakes or "people pointing at a
whiteboard" clichés.
```

**Negative prompt:** `text, caption, watermark, logo, brand name, readable UI, screen glare, dashboard, chart graphics, hologram, glow, lens flare, floating icons, 3d render, cgi, illustration, cartoon, plastic skin, waxy, distorted hands, extra fingers, oversaturated, HDR halo, futuristic, neon, handshake, thumbs up`

## 2. Stock-photography fallback (if not generating)

Search Adobe Stock / Getty / Stocksy for:

`asian creative team laptop discussion bright office singapore`,

Pick a candid, naturally-lit frame with neutral tones and left/centre negative
