## 1. AI Image Generation Prompt

```
A modern, premium split-screen digital illustration for a marketing agency blog,
1600x900, 16:9 aspect ratio, clean editorial style, not a generic stock photo.

LEFT HALF — "Organic Social Media": soft natural blue and white color palette,
stylized mobile phone mockups showing Facebook and Instagram post cards with
subtle like, comment, and share icons, small engagement bubbles, a gentle
upward growth line suggesting community and brand growth. Warm, human,
approachable feel.

RIGHT HALF — "Paid Social Media": a stylized Meta Ads Manager-style dashboard
panel, clean analytics bar and line charts, simple target-audience icons
(silhouette with a targeting ring), ad campaign card mockups, subtle
conversion/funnel iconography suggesting lead generation. Sharper, more
data-driven, structured feel.

The two halves meet at a soft vertical divide in the center, blended with a
subtle glow in brand cyan (#02BAF9), not a hard line.

BACKGROUND (behind both halves): a faint, softly blurred modern Singapore
skyline silhouette (Marina Bay-style towers) at dusk, layered with subtle
translucent technology/network line patterns (thin connecting dots and
lines), low opacity so it stays a background texture and never competes with
the foreground icons.

COMPOSITION: generous clean negative space in the upper third and center of
the image, free of icons or dense detail, reserved for a text/title overlay
to be added separately. Balanced, uncluttered, high-end digital agency
illustration style — think premium SaaS marketing site aesthetic (Stripe,
Linear-adjacent), not clip-art, not photorealistic stock photography.

COLOR PALETTE: primary cyan #02BAF9, white, soft light-blue tints, black/
charcoal for contrast and UI-style elements. Minimal, confident, professional.

STRICT CONSTRAINTS: no readable/legible body text or paragraphs anywhere in
the image (icon labels and UI chrome should be abstract/illegible-scale
only); no human faces in close-up; no generic stock-photo clichés (no
handshakes, no generic "people pointing at a whiteboard"); no clutter; no
watermarks or logos other than abstract app-style UI chrome.
```

**Negative prompt (if supported by the generation tool):** `stock photo, generic office, handshake, watermark, logo, legible paragraph text, cluttered, low quality, photorealistic faces, busy background`
