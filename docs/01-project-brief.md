## 2. Project Vision
**Status: Mixed — see labels**

**Confirmed positioning (from the live site):** Envis8ge presents itself as a fuss-free, transparent, data-informed social media and digital marketing partner for regional brands, combining creativity with data-driven insight.

**Proposed vision for this redesign** *(recommendation, requires client approval)*:

Because Envis8ge sells marketing and digital presence as a service, its own website is a direct, visible proof point of its capability — prospective clients will implicitly judge the agency's skill by the quality of its own site. On that basis, the recommended vision is:

> A redesigned website that itself functions as a working example of Envis8ge's craft — fast, credible, content-driven, and conversion-focused — while clearly and simply communicating the agency's services, evidence of results, and ease of getting in touch.

This vision statement is a proposal only. It has not been validated with the client and should be replaced or confirmed once the actual motivation for the redesign (e.g. repositioning, underperforming leads, credibility gap, rebrand) is known.

**To be confirmed:** The client's own stated reason for undertaking this redesign, and whether the boutique/regional positioning should be preserved, scaled up, or repositioned.

## 12. Risks & Assumptions
**Status: Proposed — to be reviewed with stakeholders**

**Risks:**

- **Scope ambiguity:** Business objectives, success metrics, and much of the functional scope are undefined (see sections above). Proceeding to design/build without closing these gaps risks rework.
- **Content availability:** No case study details, results data, testimonials, or team bios currently exist. Portfolio and credibility-building sections depend on content that has not yet been sourced.
- **Client/logo permissions:** Reuse of existing client names/logos in a new design has not been confirmed as permitted.
- **SEO regression:** Restructuring the site (URLs, content) without a migration/redirect plan risks losing existing search visibility.
- **Brand inconsistency:** No brand guidelines or design system exist yet; design work started before these are defined risks rework.
- **Timeline/budget unknowns:** No project timeline or budget has been documented, making effort estimation unreliable at this stage.

**Assumptions:**

- The redesign covers the public marketing website only, unless stakeholders indicate otherwise.
- The site will remain single-market/English-language unless multi-language support is explicitly requested.
- The six services currently listed remain accurate until the client states otherwise.
- The existing domain and business contact details remain in use unless told otherwise.

All risks and assumptions above should be reviewed and either confirmed or corrected by the client before proceeding past the documentation phase.
