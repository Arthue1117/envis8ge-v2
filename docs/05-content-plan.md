# Content Plan

> **How to read this document.** Same convention as prior locked documents:
> - **Confirmed** — real copy/content that exists on the live site today and must be migrated, not rewritten.
> - **Approved addition** — new content required for features already approved (Blog, Portfolio Detail).
> - **Proposed** — a content recommendation requiring sign-off.
> - **To be confirmed** — content that does not exist yet and must come from the client — never invented.
>
> **Governing principle (per your explicit direction):** this is a rebuild, not a rewrite. Existing copy is migrated verbatim wherever it is confirmed. Nothing below fabricates client results, quotes, or statistics.

---

## 1. Content Principles

- Preserve existing messaging exactly where it is confirmed (Section 2) — no rewriting for style unless you request it
- New content (Blog, Portfolio Detail narratives) follows the Voice & Tone rules in the locked [02-brand-guidelines.md](02-brand-guidelines.md) Section 4
- No invented statistics, client results, testimonials, or history anywhere in new content
- Every content gap below is flagged for the client to fill — not guessed at
